Buy well. Buy once.

Before you order online

  • Compare the total after tax, shipping, delivery, assembly, and installation.
  • Check the actual seller, warranty, return window, return shipping, and restocking fees.
  • Confirm opened or assembled-item returns and the damage-reporting deadline.
  • Know whether delivery is curbside, doorway, room-of-choice, or assembled.
  • Check condition: new, refurbished, returned, or open-box. Keep receipts.

For the comfort purchases

  • Try mattresses, pillows, sofas, recliners, dining chairs, and office chairs.
  • See rug, upholstery, and curtain samples in person.
  • Check artwork, exercise equipment, and cookware weight and grip.
  • Record the exact manufacturer and model number before comparing prices.
  • Compare the whole service package for large, heavy, fragile, or installed items.
Buying second-hand

Usually reasonable after inspection: solid-wood tables, dining chairs, bookshelves, metal shelving, mirrors, patio furniture, desks, non-upholstered benches, reputable-brand tools, and plant pots.

Prefer new: mattresses, pillows, upholstered furniture with unknown history, surge protectors, smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms, extinguishers, space heaters, extension cords, helmets, and safety equipment. Avoid damaged nonstick coatings and heavily worn appliances without service history. Check product recalls.

When local purchasing makes more sense

Buy locally for same-day needs, comfort tests, fragile or extremely heavy items, exact color matching, installation support, expensive return shipping, complicated delivery access, or second-hand inspections. Online order with store pickup can combine price comparison with faster collection.

The simplest shopping schedule

Before moving: order bedding, towels, bathroom and kitchen starters, cleaning and laundry supplies, bins, tools, emergency lighting, surge protectors, and safety supplies. Make one store visit to test the mattress, pillows, sofa, dining chair, and office chair.

After two weeks: bed frame, dining table, sofa, curtains, entryway storage, closet organizers, office setup, and more cookware. After one month: rug, coffee table, media storage, extra linens, guest supplies, regularly used small appliances, and storage based on actual clutter. After three months: consider premium furniture, larger decor, smart-home products, guest-room furniture, exercise equipment, outdoor setup, and specialized appliances.
