climate and weather

Puerto Vallarta is a good winter beach climate in regional climatology, not a guaranteed forecast: WeatherSpark shows January average highs around 81°F and lows around 62°F, December highs around 82°F and lows around 64°F, with roughly 1–2 rain days in December/January and a much wetter June–October season. The official tourism page describes winter averages in the mid-80s and the rainy season mainly June–October. Morning/evening layers are useful, and a boat/snorkel day should have an indoor or land-based alternative. No December 2026 forecast or sea-state guarantee is claimed.

neighborhoods and lodging

The priced hotel is Comfort Inn Puerto Vallarta Golfside & Marina near the airport, with breakfast and a public description placing the airport under five minutes away.
