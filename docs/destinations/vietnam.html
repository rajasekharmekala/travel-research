Climate and weather

Official Vietnam Tourism climatology distinguishes regions: HCMC/Mekong is generally clear, hot and sunny December-April; Phu Quoc is generally clear, hot and sunny October-June; Hoi An is rainy/cool/cloudy September-January; Hue is rainy/cloudy September-February; and Hanoi is cool/cold/misty/cloudy December-March. This supports a southern route and makes a central Vietnam beach extension a poor fit for this window. These are climatological patterns, not a Dec 2026 forecast; sea conditions, storms and heat can still disrupt activities.

Experiences and closures

The experience thesis is a split southern trip: HCMC for street food, markets, museums, architecture and a Mekong/Cu Chi day; Phu Quoc for beach time, a night market, snorkeling only if conditions permit and low-key resort days. Neighborhoods and lodging

In HCMC, District 1 is the practical first base for Ben Thanh Market, central museums, the riverfront and dense food options; District 3 can be quieter and food-forward but adds short rides. On Phu Quoc, Duong To/Long Beach is convenient for the airport and broad lodging choice, Duong Dong is useful for the night market and local food, while An Thoi/Sunset Town is more resort-oriented and farther south. Booking.com exact-date results showed HCMC promotional examples around USD 79-86/night and a Phu Quoc apartment at USD 746 for five nights, but Phu Quoc had 72% of properties unavailable, so inventory and fees are meaningful risks.

Transport and travel fatigue

The selected international candidate is a one-stop SFO-TPE-SGN with roughly 19h20 outbound and 16h30 return; it is long but materially less fatiguing and cheaper than SJC's two-stop Qatar option and OAK's two-stop Southwest/EVA option. The SGN-PQC domestic candidate is nonstop about one hour each way, but the Jan 1 return is late, so the plan uses a final HCMC night and no tight same-day international connection.
