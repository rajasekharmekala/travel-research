Advisor memo

Los Cabos is a conditional yes at about USD 6,976 for two, on a Southwest fare seen only on Skyscanner.

My recommendation

Go to San Jose del Cabo, fly out of Oakland on Southwest with one stop each way, and stay thirteen nights at Hyatt Place Los Cabos. I priced that trip at about USD 6,976 for the two of you, only about USD 24 under your firm USD 7,000 ceiling, using a Southwest fare I could see only on Skyscanner, not on Southwest's own site. A realistic range is USD 4,172 to 9,454, and the airfare is what moves it. This is a conditional yes, not a confident one.

Entry should be simple if your US stamps are valid. The Mexican consulate in San Francisco says a valid, unexpired US visa replaces a Mexican visa for tourism or transit, as long as passport and visa both cover the whole stay; the page states this generally and does not name Indian passports. There is no lead time and no last safe application date, but it rests on your own statement that both US stamps are valid through January 5, which I could not verify. Read the expiry date on each stamp first.

The one thing that flips this is the Southwest fare. Skyscanner lists it at USD 634 per person, USD 1,267 for two. Southwest.com would not show me a fare list after two tries, so I could not confirm it on the airline's own site. Alaska's site shows Oakland to San Jose del Cabo from USD 995 per person, and at that price the trip costs about USD 7,735, well over your ceiling.

Why this trip fits the two of you

You asked for beach, food and culture, winter scenery, nature and value. Los Cabos serves beach, food and a bit of culture, and the whale season serves nature. It trades away winter scenery completely: the tourism board describes December through February as pleasantly cool, around 75 degrees, with about 300 sunny days a year. That is climatology, not a forecast, but I would not pretend there is any snow to find.

San Jose del Cabo is the right base for your preferences. Three independent guides describe it the same way: quieter and older than Cabo San Lucas, with a colonial center, a gallery district and farm-to-table dinners, and little going on after 10 PM. If you want nightlife, this is the wrong town. If you want a long dinner and a gallery walk, it is the right one.

Thirteen nights on one base is long. That is the point of the window, and I built in several days with almost nothing planned, because a beach town with one hotel gets repetitive if you try to fill every day.

How the trip unfolds

Arrival, December 23. The Southwest flight leaves Oakland at 7:05 AM, stops in Santa Ana, and lands at 12:50 PM. Take the airport taxi at the official kiosk, about USD 75, check in and eat at the hotel. I would do nothing else.

The first week in San Jose. On Christmas Eve, a Thursday, walk the plaza and the 1730 mission, then the gallery district. The Thursday Art Walk runs from November through June, but whether it runs on Christmas Eve or New Year's Eve is unconfirmed, so ask. On Christmas Day take a taxi to Chileno Bay, one of the calm coves the tourism board names for swimming. The same board warns that some beaches have rip currents and undertows, so check the flag color every time: green is safe, red or black means stay out.

The hotel is Hyatt Place. I picked it because it is the one stay I could price with taxes spelled out, it includes a breakfast buffet that guests call better than most hotel breakfasts, the rate cancels free until December 21, and it is about 984 feet from a beach. The Stay guide notes a car is essentially required here, which is why I budgeted taxis. If you would rather spend about USD 710 less before any tax, Hampton Inn & Suites shows USD 128 a night on Hilton's own site with breakfast and parking, but it sits at Km 24.8 on the Transpeninsular highway in Cerro Colorado (I did not read its distance from the old town) and its tax treatment is unstated. I could not tell you whether Hyatt Place carries Chase Travel benefits; no public Chase page lists it.

Food, and a day in To­dos Santos. One day is a long lunch at Flora Farms, a 25-acre organic farm in the foothills that the tourism board credits with defining farm-to-table dining here. Another is a private-driver day to To­dos Santos, a Pueblo Magico founded in 1723 and about an hour north of Cabo San Lucas. It is the best culture day of the trip. Its Pacific beaches are for surfers, not for swimming. I assumed USD 250 for the driver because the airport rate card lists To­dos Santos as quote on request.

New Year. December 31 is a Thursday. The tourism page says restaurants prepare special menus, but that page is dated 2019, so I would not trust a venue or a price until a restaurant confirms it. Book a set-menu table and the taxi home ahead of time. January 1 is a recovery day.

The whale day, January 2. This is the nature highlight. Whale season runs December to April, with January and February the highest-encounter months, and the LivingInCabo guide puts shared tours at USD 80 to 120 per person. Nearly all of them leave from the Cabo San Lucas marina, as the Baja Pass guide points out, so I made it a Cabo San Lucas day: morning tour, then the water taxi to El Arco and Lover's Beach, then lunch on Medano. January 3 is the backup if the sea is rough.

Departure, January 5. The 3:45 PM flight via San Diego lands in Oakland at 8:25 PM.

What it will really cost

Here is the arithmetic. Flights are USD 1,447: USD 1,267 for two Southwest fares plus USD 180 for four checked bags at USD 45, which is Southwest's posted US mainland fee because I could not read a Mexico schedule. The room is USD 2,374: USD 1,935 listed for 13 nights plus 20 percent for VAT and tax plus USD 52 in environmental fees, breakfast included. Meals are USD 1,570, taxis USD 921, whale tour and water taxi USD 218, and I added USD 446.49 for an insurance placeholder and a 5 percent reserve. The total is USD 6,976.29.

Only the flight price and the room price are read from pages, and neither was confirmed on the airline's or hotel's own site. Meals, taxis and tours are estimates built from Numbeo, guide ranges and the airport taxi rate card, each with a range in the dossier. The low case is about USD 4,172; the high case is about USD 9,454, which is over your ceiling.

On value, I rate this conditional. It costs about USD 896 less than the nonstop version out of San Francisco for about four extra hours of travel in total (about 1.5 hours out and 2.3 back). That is a good trade if the fare holds.

Where this can go wrong

First, the fare. Southwest-direct did not load, so the cheap fare is unconfirmed, and the bag fee is borrowed from the mainland schedule. Second, taxes: I computed them for Hyatt Place from the exclusions the page states, not from a final total. Third, the surf. Beach flags decide your beach days, and a hotel near the water does not make the water safe.

Fourth, the holiday week. Closures, set menus, the Art Walk and taxi prices around Christmas and New Year are unconfirmed. Fifth, the pace: this is not a trip for people who need nightlife or snow, and the Baja Pass guide says activity-heavy visitors are happier in Cabo San Lucas.

Cancellation exposure is low for the room, free until December 21 and paid at the property, and unknown for the airfare because I did not read the fare rules.

The best alternative

The strongest alternative is the same room with United nonstop out of San Francisco: USD 1,060 per person from United's site, a total of about USD 7,872. It saves about four hours of flying in total (about 1.5 hours out and 2.3 back) and costs about USD 896 more, which puts it USD 872 over your ceiling, so I rate it a weak fit unless a nonstop round trip for two with bags turns up near USD 1,470. The early December 19 departure is possible, but Oakland fares for it start at USD 720 per person on a two-stop outbound and USD 1,011 on a nonstop one, plus four more hotel nights.

What to check next

1. Price Oakland to San Jose del Cabo on southwest.com for two, with bags. A total near USD 1,447 keeps the trip near USD 6,976; a price near Alaska's USD 995 per person makes it a weak fit. 2. Read both US visa stamp expiry dates. An expired stamp means a consular visa appointment before booking. 3. Confirm the Hyatt Place total and cancellation on the Booking.com checkout page or hyatt.com. 4. Ask for January 2 whale tour prices and pickup, and for New Year's Eve menus. 5. Get one rental-car quote with Mexican insurance; if five days costs under about USD 350 it beats my taxi budget.
