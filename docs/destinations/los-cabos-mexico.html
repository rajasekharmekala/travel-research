Advisor memo

Los Cabos in the San Jose del Cabo style: about USD 6,391 for two, inside your ceiling if the fares hold

My recommendation

Go to San Jose del Cabo, stay at the Hyatt Place for thirteen nights, and fly out of San Jose on Southwest with a stop each way. The trip I priced comes to about USD 6,391 for the two of you, inside your USD 7,000 ceiling by about USD 609. A realistic range is USD 5,310 to 8,950, and the airfare and bag fees are what swing it.

Entry looks simple. The San Francisco consulate says a valid US visa waives the Mexican tourist visa when passport and visa are valid for the whole stay, so there is no lead time and no last safe application date. That rests on your own statement that both US stamps are valid through Jan 5, which I could not verify. Check each stamp's expiry date first.

The one thing that would flip this is the airline-direct total. If Southwest's real round trip plus bags comes in more than about USD 600 above the USD 1,547 I used, the cushion is gone and the SFO nonstop stops looking expensive by comparison.

Why this trip fits the two of you

You asked for beach, food and culture, winter scenery, nature and value. Los Cabos serves food and culture and the beach, and the whale season covers nature. It trades away winter scenery completely: days are about 75F and dry, and there is no snow. I would not pretend otherwise.

San Jose del Cabo is the right base for your preferences. A regional guide calls it quieter, older and more authentic than Cabo San Lucas, with a colonial center, an art district and farm-to-table dining. The same guide says little happens after 10 PM. If you want nightlife, this is the wrong town; if you want a long dinner and a gallery walk, it is the right one.

Thirteen nights is long for one base. That is the point of the window, and I built in a few days with nothing booked, because a beach town with one hotel gets repetitive if you try to fill every day.

How the trip unfolds

Arrival, December 23. You land mid-afternoon after about five hours door to door with a connection in San Diego. Take the airport taxi, around USD 75 at the official kiosk, check in and eat at the hotel. Do nothing else.

The first week is the old town and the coves. On Christmas Eve I would walk the plaza and the mission, then the four blocks of galleries on Calle Obregon. The Thursday Art Walk keeps galleries open until about 9 PM from November to June, but whether it runs on Christmas Eve is unconfirmed, so ask. On Christmas Day, take a taxi to Palmilla or Chileno Bay. A resident-style guide says the swimmable beaches are those coves, five to ten minutes away, not the surf-battered stretches. The tourism board is blunt that some beaches have rip currents; check the flag color every time.

Midweek, I would go to Cabo San Lucas twice. The first trip is the water taxi to the Arch and Lover's Beach, around USD 9 each. The second, on December 30, is a sunset cruise from USD 68.90 each. One guide notes that nearly every tour leaves from that marina, which is why I would not base you there but would visit.

New Year's Eve is a Thursday. The tourism board talks about special menus and parties, but its page is dated 2019, so I would not trust a venue or a price until a restaurant confirms. Book a set-menu table and plan the taxi home in advance. January 1 is a recovery day.

The best experience comes on January 2: a small-group whale watch with a marine biologist, listed from USD 109 each. Whale season runs December to April, and a resident guide says January and February have the highest encounter rates. No sighting is guaranteed, so I kept January 3 free as a backup. January 4 is packing and a last dinner, and January 5 is a one-stop flight home arriving at 6:45 PM.

What it will really cost

Here is the arithmetic. Flights are USD 1,547: Skyscanner showed USD 1,347 for two on Southwest via San Diego both ways, plus a USD 200 bag allowance that I borrowed from a United fee I observed. The room is USD 2,239.60: USD 1,823 for 13 nights before tax, plus 20 percent for VAT and tax, plus USD 52 in environmental fees, with breakfast included. Meals are USD 1,300, taxis USD 350, tours USD 373.80, and I added a 10 percent reserve of USD 581.04. The total is USD 6,391.44.

Only the airfare and the room are read from rendered pages. Meals, taxis, tours and the reserve are estimates, each with a range in the dossier. The low case, with every line at its low end, is about USD 5,310; the high case is about USD 8,950, and that is over your ceiling.

On value, I score this a conditional fit. It is cheaper by about USD 842 than the nonstop version for roughly two extra hours of travel each way. That is a good trade for you if the fares hold.

Where this can go wrong

First, fares. I could not read Southwest's return fare or its bag fees, and Alaska's site dropped the dates twice, so no airline-direct total exists. Second, entry on the US side: I could not read the State Department or CBP pages, so re-entry rests on your statement. Third, the surf. Beach flags decide your beach days and a hotel near the water does not make the water safe.

Fourth, holiday week. Closures, set menus and taxi prices around Christmas and New Year are unconfirmed. Fifth, the pace: this is not a trip for people who need nightlife or snow.

Cancellation exposure is low for the room, which is free to cancel until December 21 and paid at the property. It is higher for the airfare, because Basic fares are usually restrictive and I did not read the fare rules.

The best alternative

The strongest alternative is the same hotel with a nonstop out of San Francisco on Alaska, USD 2,112 for two plus bags, for a total of about USD 7,233. It saves about two hours each way and costs about USD 842 more. It is over your ceiling by about USD 233, so I rate it a weak fit unless the fare drops to about USD 1,700 or you raise the ceiling.

A second alternative is staying in Cabo San Lucas. A guide says it is better for tours and nightlife. I would pick it only if you want boats and parties more than galleries.

What to check next

1. Confirm both US visa stamps are valid through January 5. If either is not, stop and resolve it before anything else. 2. On Southwest.com, search SJC to SJD December 23 to January 5 for two, and read the return fare and bag fees. If the total with bags is within about USD 600 of USD 1,547, the plan stands. 3. Open the Hyatt Place Los Cabos site and compare its rate to USD 1,823 before tax. Also look at the Chase Travel portal yourself, since I did not sign in. 4. Ask venues about Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve hours and menus. 5. Check a 10-day forecast and a licensed whale operator before January 2.
