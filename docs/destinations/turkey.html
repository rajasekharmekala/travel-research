Entry and transit

Türkiye MFA states ordinary Indian passport holders require a visa; a qualifying valid Schengen, USA, UK or Ireland visa or residence permit may allow a single-entry e-Visa subject to conditions. The page advises passport validity at least six months from arrival and describes an additional validity rule beyond the visa duration. The travelers' exact supporting documents are unknown, so this is a decision gate. The US State Department return page was blocked by Cloudflare; general CBP guidance says admissibility is decided by CBP inspection, so H-1B visa stamps, I-797 and partner documents must be confirmed independently. Istanbul is a single-airport itinerary with no self-transfer, but no remote-work permission is assumed.
