climate and weather

December Vienna climatology is cold and low-light: Weather Spark shows typical highs falling about 41F to 37F, lows 32F to 28F, about an 18% measurable-precipitation day chance and roughly 8h20m daylight around the solstice. Snow is possible but not guaranteed; Alpine/Munich conditions can be colder and more snow-sensitive. This is regional long-run climatology, not a forecast for [calendar date omitted]26. Plan indoor museums, cafés, markets and palace interiors, with outdoor/Alpine days weather-dependent.
