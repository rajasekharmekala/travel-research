entry and transit

The official EU guidance says non-EU nationals need a valid passport and possibly a visa; Austria and Germany are Schengen countries, and common short-stay rules also cover airport transit. Passport validity, visa/residence-permit status, proof of lodging/return and sufficient means must be checked for both travelers. The reported Indian passport nationality does not prove visa-free entry. Any Toronto, Istanbul, Dublin or other connection has its own transit-document rules that were not established here. USCIS guidance says H-1B travelers return using H-1B documents, but the user's visa-stamp validity is null and partner status is unknown. These are decision gates, not assumptions.

climate and weather

December Vienna climatology is cold and low-light: Weather Spark shows typical highs falling about 41F to 37F, lows 32F to 28F, about an 18% measurable-precipitation day chance and roughly 8h20m daylight around the solstice. Snow is possible but not guaranteed; Alpine/Munich conditions can be colder and more snow-sensitive. This is regional long-run climatology, not a forecast for Dec 2026. Plan indoor museums, cafés, markets and palace interiors, with outdoor/Alpine days weather-dependent.

experiences and closures

Vienna offers the best dated holiday continuity: Rathausplatz and Stephansplatz run through Dec 26, Schönbrunn and Vienna Ice World through Jan 6, and Belvedere through Dec 31; Freyung, Am Hof, Karlsplatz and Spittelberg close Dec 23. Munich's official guide lists the main Marienplatz market Nov 20-Dec 24, with smaller food/winter markets continuing later. Prioritize Vienna's markets before/around Christmas, Schönbrunn/Prater after Christmas, museums, palace interiors, cafés, classical music and a weather-flexible Wachau or Salzburg/Alpine day only after transport checks. Christmas/New Year hours can override the normal itinerary.
