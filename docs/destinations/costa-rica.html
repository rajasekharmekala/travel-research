Climate and weather

December should be planned from regional climatology, not a distant forecast. Costa Rica's meteorological institute explains that climate statistics require long observation periods and provides separate regional analysis for the Central Valley, Northern Zone, Pacific and Caribbean regions. A La Fortuna plus Pacific itinerary has asymmetric weather exposure: road and volcano-region activity can face cloud or rain while Pacific beach days may be more favorable. Daylight is shorter than summer and conditions can change quickly near highland volcano areas. No reliable 2026-12 forecast is available yet.

Experiences and closures

The strongest experience mix is Arenal/La Fortuna for volcano scenery, hot springs and nature, followed by a Pacific base for beach, food and a slower finish. SINAC's conservation-area directory confirms official protected-area structures including Arenal, Guanacaste, Pacífico Central and Tempisque, but the rendered page did not show December 2026 hours or closures. Reserve only after checking holiday schedules, park tickets, trail conditions, weather cancellations and operator cancellation terms. Rainy-day alternatives include hot springs, museums/coffee experiences and flexible food days rather than unsafe road improvisation.

Transport and travel fatigue

SJO is Costa Rica's main international gateway; LIR is the more direct gateway for Guanacaste, Monteverde and some La Fortuna access. SFO is the primary priced origin because the browser route context could be established there; OAK and SJC were not assigned unsupported price claims. A connection from the Bay Area plus a same-day road transfer to La Fortuna creates fatigue, so the first night should be low-demand. Use licensed/official transfers or a reputable rental arrangement after confirming insurance and road conditions; do not treat self-transfers as guaranteed savings. The preferred itinerary keeps the arrival and return on a single gateway rather than mixing airports.

Risks and cancellation

The largest risks are not just weather: holiday capacity and minimum stays, fare volatility, road delays, incomplete visa/re-entry information, employer restrictions on remote work, and hotel taxes/cancellation conditions. A connection can create missed-connection exposure, while a split stay can make a short trip feel rushed. Select refundable or changeable components where price differences are acceptable, keep the first day light, and do not buy a nonrefundable itinerary before documents are resolved. Security-verification blockers on official sites are recorded rather than bypassed.

Alternatives and pushback

The strongest competing itinerary is LIR plus Guanacaste beach with fewer road transfers, but it weakens the volcano/nature mix and needs a separate LIR fare check. A single-base La Fortuna plan reduces hotel switching but sacrifices beach time; a 14-night window (return 2027-01-06) improves pace but adds lodging, food and remote-work/document exposure. The 2026-12-19 departure windows may reduce post-arrival pressure but are remote-work dependent and add holiday nights. Pushback: Costa Rica is not automatically cheap in Christmas/New Year week, and the current estimate is not evidence that the budget will hold. If exact airfare is high, a shorter one-region plan may preserve value better than adding destinations.
